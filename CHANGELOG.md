# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Gear, cog, ratio, Di2 battery and learned battery range
- Cassette background graphic; chainring and cog drawn to scale in the Cog view
- D-FLY button switches views (short / long / double press)
- Stats in Garmin Connect: gear, cog and ratio charts; % time per gear, avg/max ratio, shifts, battery
- Two bike profiles (cassette and chainrings per bike)
- Supports 15 Edge models (Explore 2/3, 530/830/1030, 540/840/1040, 550/850/1050/1060, MTB)
- Low battery warning at 10% (adjustable): tone once per ride, red label while low
- Last gear stays visible in grey for 30 s after a short connection drop
- "Searching" screen with a shifter illustration
- 500 km free trial, then a one-time license (e-mail + code in the app settings)
- 26 languages: all European languages supported by the Edge, plus Russian and Chinese
