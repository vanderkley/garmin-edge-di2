# Changelog

## 1.0.1

- Battery range shows an estimate right away (Shimano: about 1000 km per charge) and adapts
  to your own rides in the background, instead of showing "learning" first
- Friendlier start screen: "Shift or press the Di2 button" in the field's own font, and
  "Connecting Di2…" once the Di2 is found
- Edge Explore 2 firmware 31.33 (Bluetooth bug): the field now shows the Di2 battery and range
  instead of only "Edge firmware not supported" (range below the battery %, as D-FLY is blocked too)
- Demos no longer show the searching screen
- Free trial is now 50 km (was 500 km). The remaining trial distance is shown like in BuienRit and
  WindRit: before the ride and while paused, 20 s after the timer starts, and every minute in the last 10 km
- License price: 8 EUR, ordered on buienrit.nl/di2 (the lock screen shows that address)

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Gear, cog, ratio, Di2 battery and learned battery range
- Cassette background graphic; chainring and cog drawn to scale in the Cog view
- D-FLY button switches views (short / long / double press)
- Stats in Garmin Connect: gear, cog and ratio charts; % time per gear, avg/max ratio, shifts, battery
- Two bike profiles (cassette and chainrings per bike)
- Supports 15 Edge models (Explore 2/3, 530/830/1030, 540/840/1040, 550/850/1050/1060, MTB)
- Low battery warning at 10% (adjustable): tone once per ride, red label while low
- Last gear stays visible in grey for 30 s after a short connection drop
- "Searching" screen with a shifter illustration
- 500 km free trial, then a one-time license (e-mail + code in the app settings)
- 26 languages: all European languages supported by the Edge, plus Russian and Chinese
